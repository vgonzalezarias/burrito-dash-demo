How we work

Burrito Dash is a catering kitchen. We're not a counter you queue at — we cook for groups, wrap everything, and bring it to where your people are.

Orders are made for your date. The meat gets cooked, the burritos get rolled and wrapped, and the salsas, cilantro, onion and limes go into their own containers so nothing goes soggy on the way. It arrives in foil trays and large bags, with plates, napkins and utensils, and it's ready to put straight on a table.

The menu stays short on purpose. Carne asada, birria, chicharrón, picadillo, deshebrada, bistec in red chili, and a full breakfast line-up. Those are things worth doing properly rather than forty things done halfway.

We take catering orders at least two weeks out, with a 25-person minimum, because that's what it takes to cook this much food without cutting corners on any of it.
