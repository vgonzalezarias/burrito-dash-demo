Questions, answered

The things people ask before they book. If yours isn't here, just send it over.

How far in advance do I need to order?

At least two weeks. Catering orders have to be placed a minimum of two weeks before your date so the food can be bought and cooked properly for your head count. Availability is limited, so for holidays and busy weekends, earlier is better.

Is there a minimum order?

Yes, catering starts at 25 people. If you're feeding fewer than that, ask about the packs of 6 and 10 burritos, which come with chips and salsa and don't need the two-week lead time.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Delivery is available. Orders arrive in foil trays and large bags, with everything packed so it's ready to go straight onto a table.

What comes with a catering order?

Red and green salsa, cilantro, onion and limes, plus plates, napkins and utensils. There's no separate charge for serveware — it's part of the order.

Can I mix different fillings in one order?

Yes. You can mix across the menu in a single order. Tell us the split you want when you send your request — for example half carne asada and half birria — and we'll build it that way.

Can beans and rice be served separate?

Yes. On the big lunch and dinner burritos, beans and rice can be served on the side instead of rolled in, if you'd rather let people build their own plates.

How does my date get reserved?

Your date is reserved once the order is confirmed. Asking about a date doesn't hold it, and availability really is limited — so if your event is fixed, confirming early is how you keep it.

What meals do you cater?

Breakfast, lunch and dinner. Breakfast is potatoes chorizo and eggs, eggs and hotlinks, potatoes bacon and eggs, or chicharrón. Lunch and dinner are carne asada or birria, both with beans and rice.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on your head count, the meal and the fillings, so each order is quoted rather than sold off a fixed list. Send your date and guest count and you'll get a price back.

What areas do you deliver to?

We deliver across [SERVICE AREA] and the surrounding towns. If you're outside that, send your address anyway and we'll tell you straight.

Still have questions?

Send them with your date and head count and you'll get answers and a price in one reply.
