How ordering works

  1. Send the details

    Date, time, head count, and whether you want breakfast, lunch or dinner.

  2. Pick the fillings

    Choose from the menu. You can mix fillings within one order.

  3. Confirm to reserve

    We send a price. The date is held once the order is confirmed, not before.

  4. We deliver

    Hot, in foil trays and large bags, with salsa, toppings and all the serveware.

What shows up at your door

Burritos arrive wrapped and packed in foil trays, with the salsas and toppings in their own containers and the plates, napkins and utensils stacked alongside. You put it on a table and people serve themselves.

If you'd rather keep beans and rice out of the burrito so people can build their own plates, ask — we can serve them separate.

The rules, up front

  • Two weeks in advance. Catering orders must be placed at least two weeks before the date.
  • 25 people minimum on catering orders. Below that, ask about the packs of 6 and 10.
  • Limited availability. Dates are reserved once your order is confirmed — asking about a date doesn't hold it.
  • Delivery available. In trays and in large bags.

Let's get your date on the calendar

Send the head count and the day. You'll get a price back, and the date is yours once you confirm.
