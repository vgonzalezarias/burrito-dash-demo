Privacy policy

Short version: we use what you send to quote your catering order, and nothing else.

Last updated: [DATE THIS GOES LIVE]

What we collect

When you fill in the request form we collect the name, phone number, email address, event date, guest count and any notes you choose to give us. We do not collect payment details through this website.

Why we collect it

To reply to your request, quote your order, and arrange delivery. That's it. We do not sell your information, rent it, or pass it to anyone who isn't involved in getting your food to you.

How long we keep it

We keep order enquiries for as long as we need them for our own records and for repeat customers who come back next year. Ask us to delete yours and we will.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not set tracking cookies and does not run advertising trackers. [IF YOU ADD GOOGLE ANALYTICS OR A FACEBOOK PIXEL LATER, SAY SO HERE]

Third parties

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives the request for those font files. If the contact form is connected to a form service such as Netlify Forms or Formspree, your submission passes through that service on the way to us.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us through the details on the contact page.

A note from whoever built this site

This is a plain-language starting point written to match how this business actually works. It is not legal advice. If Burrito Dash starts taking payments on the site, running ads, or operating somewhere with specific privacy law, have someone qualified review it.
