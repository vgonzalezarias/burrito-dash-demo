## Client-owned images (`images/site/`)

Everything else in `images/site/` comes from Burrito Dash's own Facebook graphics, photos and TikTok screenshots in `images/fb/`. I cropped them, resized them to 1600px wide at most, removed the Facebook lightbox buttons that were baked into the screenshots, and saved them as JPG and WebP at quality 80. The logo (`images/site/logo.png`) is cropped from `images/fb/logo-transparent.png` and palette-optimised. Ownership of these images stays with the client.
